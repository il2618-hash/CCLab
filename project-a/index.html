Lost Drago

Scientific Name:

Aeshnidae Dragonfly

Discovered by / at:

An ambitious artist who tries to achieve mastery at Zen painting.

Family:

Libellulidae

Habitat:

  • Ponds
  • Lakes
  • Rivers or streams
  • Wetlands

Appearance:

A green body, black eyes with white pupils, six stretchy legs, and two huge wings.

Key Characteristics:

  • Flies in the dark.
  • Flies with fast flapping wings.
  • Bad with directions.
  • Scare of ink.

What we know so far about Drago? Drago is a special species of dragonfly that exists in the deep bamboo forests. Most commonly seen in anicent Chinese bamboo ecosystems. Young Dragos live entirely underwater so they don't dryout or freeze solid. Once they transform into flying adults, they will enter bamboo forests to hunt for flying insects such as mosquitoes, gnats, and flies.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Dragonflies has no harm to the human, in fact, they help human eliminate pests. Usually we can only interact with them by observation. However, Drago has accidentally entered the wrong dimension of a bamboo forest. Move your mouse to interact with Drago, and have fun!
